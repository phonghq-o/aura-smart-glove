Physical Architecture

Engineering Behind the Glove

Engineered specifically for low power consumption, ergonomic all-day wearable comfort, and deterministic latency.

01

Bionic Flex Sensor Array

5 high-durability bidirectional flex sensor strips mounted on custom 3D-printed articulated phalange rings. Accurately measures finger flexion and extension across 50 FPS sampling window.

  • ✓ 5x Dual-Axis Resistance Ribs
  • ✓ QMI8658 6-Axis IMU (Wrist Motion)
  • ✓ Flexible Silicone Ring Guides
02

Dual-Core Edge TinyML Hub

Driven by Espressif ESP32-S3 Xtensa LX7 dual-core processor with vectorized instructions. Executes a quantized C-LSTM neural network directly in local SRAM with 0% cloud dependency for gesture detection.

  • ✓ 240 MHz Dual-Core Processing
  • ✓ Int8 Quantized C-LSTM Model
  • ✓ Inference Latency < 15 ms
03

AMOLED & Full-Duplex Audio

LilyGO 1.9" AMOLED high-contrast touch screen paired with an ES8311 I2S audio codec, onboard microphone, and compact speaker for direct vocal conversation with anyone.

  • ✓ 368 × 448 Retina AMOLED Screen
  • ✓ ES8311 24-bit Hi-Fi Voice I2S Codec
  • ✓ MicroSD Slot for 3D Sign Avatars
Bilateral Interaction

How Two-Way Communication Works

True accessibility requires dialogue, not a one-way monologue. AURA bridges both directions seamlessly.

DIRECTION 1: I SIGN (TÔI KÝ HIỆU)

From Hand Motion to Natural Voice

The user signs with the glove. Flex sensors and IMU detect angular deflection in real-time. The on-device TinyML model classifies the gesture, while Gemini AI synthesizes a fluid, empathetic sentence broadcasted via the wrist speaker.

1. Finger Flex Sensors 50 FPS Raw Data
2. C-LSTM On-Device TinyML Offline <15ms
3. Gemini AI Sentence Synthesizer Contextual Speech
4. ES8311 Audio Speaker Vocalized Speech
DIRECTION 2: I LISTEN (TÔI LẮNG NGHE)

From Spoken Words to Sign Avatars

The hearing partner speaks into the AURA wrist microphone. Speech is transcribed and simplified by Gemini STT in real-time, then displayed as clear text and 3D animated sign language avatar animations on the AMOLED screen.

1. Wrist Microphone (ES8311) 16kHz PCM Audio
2. Cloud STT & Text Simplifier Gemini 1.5 Flash
3. MicroSD 3D Sign Avatars 30-60 FPS Video
4. High-Contrast AMOLED Display Visual Understanding
