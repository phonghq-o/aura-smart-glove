Over 70 million deaf individuals worldwide remain isolated in everyday communication. A.U.R.A. is an intelligent bionic glove engineered with On-Device TinyML. It translates sign gestures into discrete spoken words via an onboard speaker and interprets ambient speech into real-time visual sign cues on the wrist display — 100% autonomous, private, and camera-free.

01 // THE BREAKTHROUGH

Full-Duplex Acoustic & Visual Bridge

Eliminating external cameras and restrictive lighting conditions. AURA translates hand kinematics into clear acoustic words while simultaneously transcribing surrounding conversation into visual signs on the wrist AMOLED.

1

On-Device TinyML Inference

Quantized lightweight neural network executes locally on-device. Translates discrete sign gestures into discrete spoken words in <15ms without cloud dependencies.

2

Hi-Fi Acoustic Vocalization

Integrated audio system delivers clear spoken pronunciation through an ergonomic wrist-mounted micro-speaker.

3

100% Privacy & Dark Operability

Pure biomechanical resistive sensing functions flawlessly in pitch darkness, rain, and crowded public venues without invasive video capture.

NOTICE: Displayed hardware represents the working laboratory engineering prototype. Commercial release will integrate seamless injection-molded chassis and internal flex traces.

03 // HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE

Bionic Hardware Anatomy

Engineered at HCMUT: Dual-Core embedded computing, 5-finger flex sensor arrays, and biocompatible phalange fabrication.

01
COMPUTING HUB

ESP32-S3 + ESP32-C6

Main computation is powered by the ESP32-S3 dual-core processor running at 240 MHz for fast on-device AI gesture recognition. It pairs with an auxiliary coprocessor managing low-power Bluetooth 5.3 and Wi-Fi mesh communication.
< 15 ms Inference Latency
240 MHz Dual-Core Processor
Bluetooth 5.3 & Wi-Fi Sync
02
SENSORY MATRIX

5-Finger Flex Array

5 bidirectional flex resistance strips mounted along ergonomic 3D-printed phalange joints. Accurately tracks every finger bend angle in real time with continuous motion filtering.
5 Sensors Full Hand Tracking
Smooth Real-Time Sampling
Resin 3D Ergonomic Mounts
03
AUDIO SUBSYSTEM

Voice Speaker & Mic

Hi-Fi audio codec integrated with a miniature speaker. Pronounces spoken words loudly and clearly for conversation partners, while a sensitive microphone captures ambient speech with noise reduction.
Clear Voice Onboard Speaker
Noise Filter Ambient Mic
Full-Duplex Two-Way Sound
04
VISUAL INTERFACE

1.9" Retina AMOLED

High-resolution 368 × 448 px display with vibrant colors and deep blacks. Shows animated sign avatars, subtitles, and battery telemetry clearly even under bright outdoor sunlight.

04 // END-TO-END PIPELINE

Bidirectional Communication Architecture

Continuous data transformation bridging the barrier between the deaf and hearing worlds.

FORWARD DIRECTION Sign ⟶ Acoustic Voice
01

Biomechanical Motion Acquisition

5 flex sensors & 6-axis motion sensor stream continuous angle and velocity vectors with real-time filtering.

02

On-Device TinyML Inference

Embedded processor evaluates sensor data locally to identify discrete hand signs in <15ms.

03

Discrete Audio Vocalization

Outputs corresponding spoken words through the onboard speaker with zero cloud delay.

REVERSE DIRECTION Acoustic Voice ⟶ Visual Sign
01

Acoustic Audio Ingestion

Microphone captures surrounding conversation with noise filtering.

02

Semantic Keyword Parsing

Spoken voice is transcribed and mapped to recognizable sign language tokens.

03

Retina AMOLED Visual Rendering

Wrist AMOLED screen renders sign animations and subtitles for deaf users to read instantly.
