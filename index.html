Hardware Anatomy

Modular Architecture & Custom PCB

Engineered from scratch at HCMUT: Dual-Core embedded edge computing, 5-finger flex sensor arrays, and biocompatible fabrication.

EDGE COMPUTING

Dual-Core Computing Hub (ESP32-S3 + ESP32-C6)

Main computation is powered by the ESP32-S3 Xtensa dual-core 32-bit LX7 running at 240 MHz with dedicated vector instructions for TinyML inference. It pairs seamlessly with an ESP32-C6 RISC-V coprocessor managing low-power BLE 5.3 and Wi-Fi 6 mesh networking.

SENSING

5-Finger Flex Sensor Array

5 bidirectional flex resistance strips mounted along custom 3D-printed phalange joints. Captures precise finger flexion angles at 50 FPS with real-time Kalman filtering.

  • ✓ Biocompatible Silicone Mounts
  • ✓ 12-bit ADC Sampling Resolution
AUDIO

I2S Audio Codec & 24-Bit Mic

Hi-Fi ES8311 audio codec integrated with power amplifier and miniature speaker. Delivers crisp vocal pronunciation and captures ambient partner dialogue with noise filtering.

  • ✓ High-Efficiency Class-D Speaker
  • ✓ Wind Noise Suppressing MEMS Mic
DISPLAY

1.9" Retina AMOLED Display

Crisp 368 × 448 px resolution, infinite contrast, and custom LVGL 8.3 graphical interface for clear visibility under harsh outdoor sunlight.

  • ✓ SH8601 QSPI Display Driver
  • ✓ True-Black Energy Saving Mode
MOTION

QMI8658 6-Axis Motion IMU

6-DOF accelerometer and gyroscope measuring 3D spatial velocity and Yaw-Pitch-Roll wrist orientations for dynamic signs.

  • ✓ Drift Compensation Filter
  • ✓ Integrated Air Mouse Navigation
POWER

AXP2101 PMU & 1200mAh Li-Po Battery

Intelligent AXP2101 power management controller dynamically regulates voltage rails for all peripheral sensors and radio cores. Combined with a 1200mAh high-density Li-Po battery, it delivers over 8 hours of continuous operation with safe USB-C fast charging.

End-to-End Pipeline

Bidirectional Communication Architecture

How AURA bridges the communication gap between the deaf and hearing worlds.

FORWARD DIRECTION Sign ⟶ Spoken Voice

From Hand Kinematics to Spoken Voice

1
Biomechanical Motion Acquisition
5 flex sensors & 6-DOF IMU stream continuous angle and velocity vectors at 50 FPS with Kalman filtering.
2
On-Device TinyML Inference
ESP32-S3 executes quantized Int8 C-LSTM network locally in PSRAM to classify discrete hand sign classes.
3
Discrete Audio Vocalization
ES8311 audio codec outputs corresponding discrete PCM vocalizations through the onboard speaker with zero cloud delay.
REVERSE DIRECTION Spoken Voice ⟶ Visual Signs

From Partner Speech to Visual Signs

1
Acoustic Audio Ingestion
High-sensitivity MEMS microphone samples ambient partner speech at 16kHz with background noise reduction.
2
Semantic Keyword Parsing
Audio stream is transcribed and mapped to standardized sign language dictionary tokens.
3
Retina AMOLED Visual Rendering
Wrist AMOLED screen renders 3D sign avatar animations and clear text for deaf users to read instantly.
