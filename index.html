01 // THE BREAKTHROUGH

Full-Duplex Acoustic & Visual Bridge

Eliminating external cameras and restrictive lighting conditions. AURA translates hand kinematics into clear acoustic words while simultaneously transcribing surrounding conversation into visual signs on the wrist AMOLED.

1

On-Device TinyML Inference

Quantized Int8 C-LSTM network executed in local PSRAM. Translates discrete sign gestures into discrete spoken words in <15ms without cloud dependencies.

2

Hi-Fi Acoustic Vocalization (ES8311)

Dedicated I2S DAC with Class-D power amplifier delivers clear spoken pronunciation through an ergonomic wrist-mounted micro-speaker.

3

100% Privacy & Dark Operability

Pure biomechanical resistive sensing functions flawlessly in pitch darkness, rain, and crowded public venues without invasive video capture.

NOTICE: Displayed hardware represents the working laboratory engineering prototype. Commercial release will integrate seamless injection-molded chassis and internal flex traces.

03 // HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE

Bionic Hardware Anatomy

Custom engineered at HCMUT: Dual-Core embedded edge computing, 5-finger flex sensor arrays, and biocompatible phalange fabrication.

FABRICATION: LAB v2.4
MODULAR BUS: I2C / I2S / QSPI
01
COMPUTING HUB

ESP32-S3 + ESP32-C6

Primary computation is powered by the ESP32-S3 Xtensa dual-core 32-bit LX7 running at 240 MHz with dedicated vector instructions for TinyML inference. It pairs seamlessly with an ESP32-C6 RISC-V coprocessor managing low-power BLE 5.3 and Wi-Fi 6 mesh networking.
< 15 ms Inference Latency
240 MHz Dual-Core Xtensa
8 MB Octal PSRAM
02
SENSORY MATRIX

5-Finger Flex Array

5 bidirectional flex resistance strips mounted along custom 3D-printed phalange joints. Captures precise finger flexion angles at 50 FPS with real-time Kalman filtering and 12-bit ADC sampling resolution.
12-bit ADC Resolution
50 FPS Sampling Rate
Resin 3D Biocompatible Mounts
03
AUDIO SUBSYSTEM

ES8311 Codec + Mic

Hi-Fi ES8311 audio codec integrated with power amplifier and miniature speaker. Delivers crisp vocal pronunciation and captures ambient partner dialogue with noise filtering via high-sensitivity MEMS microphone.
16 kHz Hi-Fi Voice Output
I2S Bus Digital Audio Link
Class-D Onboard Amplifier
04
VISUAL INTERFACE

1.9" Retina AMOLED

Crisp 368 × 448 px resolution, infinite contrast, and custom LVGL 8.3 graphical interface for clear visibility under harsh outdoor sunlight with SH8601 QSPI display driver.
368 × 448 Retina Resolution
QSPI High-Speed Bus
LVGL 8.3 Dynamic UI Engine
05
SPATIAL MOTION

QMI8658 6-Axis IMU

6-DOF accelerometer and gyroscope measuring 3D spatial velocity and Yaw-Pitch-Roll wrist orientations for dynamic signs, paired with drift compensation filters.
6-DOF Accel + Gyro
Kalman Drift Compensation
I2C Fast-Mode 400kHz
06
POWER CORE

AXP2101 + 1200mAh Li-Po

Intelligent AXP2101 PMU dynamically regulates voltage rails for all peripheral sensors and radio cores. Combined with a 1200mAh high-density Li-Po battery, it delivers over 8 hours of continuous operation with safe USB-C fast charging.
8+ Hours Active Battery Life
USB Type-C Fast Charging
AXP2101 Dynamic PMU Rails
04 // END-TO-END PIPELINE

Bidirectional Communication Architecture

Continuous data transformation bridging the barrier between the deaf and hearing worlds.

FORWARD DIRECTION Sign ⟶ Acoustic Voice
01

Biomechanical Motion Acquisition

5 flex sensors & 6-DOF IMU stream continuous angle and velocity vectors at 50 FPS with discrete Kalman filtering.

02

On-Device TinyML Inference

ESP32-S3 executes quantized Int8 C-LSTM network locally in PSRAM to classify discrete hand sign classes in 11.8ms.

03

Discrete Audio Vocalization

ES8311 audio codec outputs corresponding discrete PCM vocalizations through the onboard speaker with zero cloud delay.

REVERSE DIRECTION Acoustic Voice ⟶ Visual Sign
01

Acoustic Audio Ingestion

High-sensitivity MEMS microphone samples ambient partner speech at 16kHz with background noise reduction.

02

Semantic Keyword Parsing

Audio stream is transcribed and mapped to standardized sign language dictionary tokens in real-time.

03

Retina AMOLED Visual Rendering

Wrist AMOLED screen renders sign avatar animations and clear text for deaf users to read instantly in sunlight.
